# Notion visual references — implementation notes

## S36 — Soft picture-book illustration
Role: character construction and rendering language.
- Rounded simplified childlike forms.
- Slightly irregular black marker outlines.
- Restrained facial features and gentle blush.
- Warm cream with muted pastel accents.
- Light paper grain and sparse doodles.
- Flat illustration with minimal shadow.
Avoid 3D, plastic shine, complex shadows, hard perspective, and polished corporate-vector treatment.

## S37 — Chinese mouse-doodle chat stickers
Role: informal chat-sticker energy and hand-written Chinese caption treatment.
- Loose imperfect linework.
- Uneven proportions and spontaneous doodle marks.
- Handwritten captions with variable scale and tilt, while remaining readable.
- Different emotional density and visual rhythm.

The original Notion card describes a 4×4, 16-sticker sheet. This Skill borrows the doodle language only. Final delivery remains one independent GIF per expression.

## S38 — Multilingual mouse-doodle stickers
Role: language adaptation and playful caption behavior.
- Caption language follows the user's requested language.
- Handwritten spontaneous text treatment.
- Playful expression without sacrificing readability.
